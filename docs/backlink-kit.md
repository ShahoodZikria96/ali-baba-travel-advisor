# Ali Baba Travel Advisor: Backlink Kit

Everything the team needs to build links without making mistakes. Work through it in order.
Rule one: **never buy links, join link-exchange groups or use mass directory-submission services.**
Google ignores or penalises them, and a few good links are worth more than hundreds of weak ones.

## 4. Pakistani directories and associations

Check each one is real, free (or reasonably priced) and still active before you submit. Skip any that look like spam.

- General business directories: Yellow Pages Pakistan, Pakistan business directories with a real address/phone list, Foursquare, Hotfrog, Cybo.
- City and chamber listings: your local chamber of commerce member directory (Lahore, Islamabad, Karachi), if you are or become a member.
- Travel-industry bodies: ask whether the Travel Agents Association of Pakistan (TAAP) or other trade bodies list members online, and whether you qualify.
- Airline and IATA-linked listings: if you are an accredited agent for any airline or GDS, ask for the "authorised agent" listing and the link.

## 5. Links worth earning (these move rankings)

These need a reason for someone to link, not just a form.

| Idea | Who to approach | What you offer |
|---|---|---|
| Guest article | Pakistani travel, lifestyle and education blogs | An original article from one of our guides, rewritten, with one link to the matching page |
| "Visa guide for Pakistanis" resource lists | Sites that list embassies or visa information | Our country visa pages (document checklists for 40+ countries) |
| Expert quote | Journalists and bloggers writing about visas or travel | A comment from a consultant, credited with the site name and link |
| Group tour story | Local news sites and community pages | The March 2027 Canada group tour with the CEO |
| Student and diaspora groups | University societies, community pages | Study visa and visit-visa guides |
| Partner links | Hotels, tour operators, insurance partners you work with | "Our partners" page links, and ask for a link back |
| Client stories | Happy clients with blogs or social profiles | A short written review with a link, only if they agree |

**Pages to point links at (best targets):**
- Homepage: brand links
- `/tour-packages/from-pakistan/` and the destination pages (Europe, Canada, Switzerland, Turkey, Japan, South Korea)
- `/visas/uk/`, `/visas/schengen/`, `/visas/canada/`, `/visas/usa/`
- The guides, especially "Schengen Visa Refusal", "How to Choose a Trustworthy Travel Agency" and "Europe Tour Packages from Pakistan: Planning Guide"
- `/travel-agency/` and the city pages for local links

## 6. Anchor text (the clickable words)

Keep it natural. Over a month, aim for roughly:

- 50 percent brand: "Ali Baba Travel Advisor", the plain URL
- 30 percent partial or descriptive: "this Schengen refusal guide", "visa consultants in Lahore"
- 15 percent generic: "click here", "this website"
- 5 percent exact keyword: "travel agency in Lahore"

Never use the same exact keyword anchor on many links.

>
> Thank you,
> [Name], Ali Baba Travel Advisor

Rules: personalise the first line, send 5 to 10 emails a day, never send the same text to hundreds of people, follow up once after a week, and accept a "no".

## 9. What to avoid

- Buying links, PBN networks, comment spam, forum spam.
- Fake reviews or fake guest profiles.
- Putting the site link in hundreds of unrelated directories.
- Mismatched phone numbers or addresses between listings.

## 10. Checking results

Search Console shows the sites that link to you under **Links**. Look at it every two weeks. Ask us to pull the Search Console data again to see which pages are gaining impressions after new links go live.
