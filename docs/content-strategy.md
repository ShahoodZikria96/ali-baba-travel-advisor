# Content Strategy: Ali Baba Travel Advisor (October 2026)

Based on Search Console data for 10 Sep to 7 Oct 2026 (170 clicks, 8,851 impressions, average position 11.7).

## What the data says

1. **One query is half of all impressions:** "europe tour packages from pakistan" (4,263 impressions, position 9.3). It was served by an old page that returned 404 after the site migration (impressions fell from about 200 a day to near zero by 1 Oct). Since 6 Oct it redirects to `/tour-packages/from-pakistan/europe/`. Winning this back is the single biggest traffic lever.
2. **Brand searches work** (positions 1 to 4). Non-branded clicks are almost zero.
3. **"Tour packages from Pakistan" for individual countries** (Canada, Switzerland, UK, Australia, South Korea, Japan, France, Morocco) have steady impressions at positions 5 to 15. These now have dedicated pages.
4. **Visa-question searches** (Japan visa for Pakistanis, Thailand visa, New Zealand visit visa, UK visit visa, easy way to go to Europe) appear at positions 7 to 65 with no matching page until now.
5. **Local searches** ("travel agency near me", "best travel agency in Lahore") rank around 9 to 18 and rely on the Google Business Profile and reviews more than on content.
6. **Dubai/UAE queries** (about 140 impressions) are not served: the company does not sell UAE services. Content is informational only.

## Audience

Pakistani individuals, families, couples and small business owners planning visits to Europe, Canada, the UK and East Asia. Most are first-time applicants worried about refusal, document lists and cost.
