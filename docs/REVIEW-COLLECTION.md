# Getting genuine reviews quickly

Reviews are the strongest trust and local-SEO signal, but only if they are real. Fake or invented reviews break Google's policies (they can get the Business Profile suspended), consumer-protection rules, and the trust of visitors — so the site does not contain any.

## How to collect them (2 minutes per client)
1. After a client's trip or visa outcome (approved or not — an honest review of your service is still valid), send the message below on WhatsApp with your Google review link (Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews" → copy link).
2. Ask permission before showing their name/photo on the website.
3. Add approved reviews in **Admin → Testimonials** (name, city, rating, text). Reviews submitted through the site's "Write a review" form arrive unpublished; approve them there.

## Message templates
**English:** Assalam o Alaikum {name}, thank you for choosing Ali Baba Travel Advisor. If you were happy with our service, we would be grateful for an honest review: {google-review-link}. It helps other travellers find reliable guidance. JazakAllah.

**اردو:** السلام علیکم {name}، علی بابا ٹریول ایڈوائزر کا انتخاب کرنے کا شکریہ۔ اگر آپ ہماری سروس سے مطمئن ہیں تو براہِ مہربانی اپنی سچی رائے یہاں لکھ دیں: {google-review-link}۔ اس سے دوسرے مسافروں کو رہنمائی ملتی ہے۔ جزاک اللہ۔

## Do not
Do not pay for reviews, offer discounts only for 5 stars, write reviews for clients, or copy reviews from other sites.
